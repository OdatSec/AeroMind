per-campaign `INSIGHTS_DRAFT.md` files; promotion of a draft insight into an
approved paper finding is a **human decision** and must be done by hand here.

## Promotion checklist (before adding a finding here)
- [ ] The campaign's raw bundles are production-valid (checksums, clean git, committed code).
- [ ] Clean-vs-attack denominators are honest (attempted vs valid separated).
- [ ] The claim does not exceed the evidence layer (L1 = retrieval; L2 = planner
      adoption/refusal/omission/breach; **no** physical-impact claim without L4).
- [ ] Scenario scope is respected (e.g. A02 is shared-memory EXPOSURE, not propagation).
- [ ] Caveats and prohibited overclaims from the draft are carried over.
- [ ] Reviewer concern (452A/B/C) is explicitly mapped.
